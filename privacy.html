Tresso Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Tresso is operated by Arconetix LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("we", "us").

Tresso is an app for barbershops to record services, payments and barber commission. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account: your mobile number and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider.
  • Shop details you enter: shop name, owner name, shop phone number and address.
  • Business records you enter: barbers (names, optional phone numbers, commission settings), services and prices, services performed, payments, barber payouts, corrections with their reasons, and optionally customer names and phone numbers.
  • Subscription: your plan, its status and your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. We never receive your card or payment details; Stripe handles payment on our web portal.
  • Technical: a random identifier created by the app on your phone, used only to keep records from different phones in sync. We do not collect advertising IDs, location, contacts or analytics.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: keep your shop's records, calculate commission and reports, and sync them between your phones.
  • To confirm and manage your subscription with Stripe.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the service providers below that run the app for us.

Where it is stored

Records are kept on your phone and on our database hosted by Supabase (cloud infrastructure in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1)). Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each shop's records are isolated: only that shop's own members can access them, according to their role (owner, manager, receptionist).

Service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Stripe (subscriptions and payments). Stripe receives your shop name and phone number to bill the subscription.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed from our database immediately; copies in backups are removed within 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Deleting an owner account permanently deletes the shop and all its records. Deleting a staff account removes that login; the records it entered stay with the shop because they belong to the business. You can also delete it on the web portal, or ask us by email; see how to delete your account. Deleting an owner account cancels the shop's subscription.

Your choices

You can view and correct your records in the app at any time, change the language, and delete your account. Contact us to ask for a copy of your data.

Children

Tresso is a business app for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
