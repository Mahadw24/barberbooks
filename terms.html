Prices

A shop needs an active subscription to use Tresso. You can pay:

  • Monthly: PKR 2,300 a month
  • Yearly: PKR 25,900 a year

You pay in advance, in Pakistani rupees, on the web dashboard. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number. Your bank may add its own fees for international card payments. If we change prices we will tell you at least 30 days before, and the new price applies from your next renewal.

Renewal and cancellation

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel. Cancel anytime on the web dashboard under Settings → Subscription → Manage billing. The shop keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

Refunds

Payments are not refundable, and we do not refund unused time in a month or year you have cancelled. If we charged you by mistake, for example twice for the same period, we refund the mistaken charge in full: email us within 30 days.

Missed payments

If a renewal fails, Stripe tries the card again and the shop keeps working for 7 days. After that the shop is locked until the payment goes through. Your records are kept and come back when it does.

Your shop's records belong to you. How we handle them is in the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account at any time; deleting an owner account also cancels the subscription.
