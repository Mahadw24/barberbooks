Delete your Tresso account

Tresso is made by Arconetix LLC.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tresso and go to Settings. (If the app says the shop has no active subscription, the button is at the bottom of that screen.)
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. You need an internet connection.

Subscription: deleting an owner account cancels the shop's subscription, so you are not charged again.

On the web

Sign in on the Tresso portal with your mobile number and password, then choose Delete account and confirm twice.

By email

If you cannot use the app, email barberbooks.support@gmail.com from any address and include the mobile number you signed up with. We will confirm with you and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Owner account: your login and your whole shop: shop details, barbers, services, customers, every service record, payment, payout and change history. Your staff lose access.
  • Staff account (manager or receptionist): your login. Records you entered stay with the shop, because they belong to the business, and are no longer linked to you.

What is kept

Nothing in our database. Backup copies are removed within 30 days. Google keeps its own record of Play purchases under Google's privacy policy.
